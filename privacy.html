Privacy and data use

Published September 9, 2026

Purpose and scope

OpenClaw Private Assistant is an owner-operated, personal-use application. It is not a public service and does not accept other users. This notice describes this installation, not all OpenClaw deployments.

Google information accessed

Google account information verifies that the assistant is connected to the intended owner. Gmail information is accessed only for the owner's requested email-assistant features, such as reading and organizing mail and preparing drafts. Permission changes are requested through Google's authorization process as capabilities are enabled.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials and retained personal assistant information are stored in the owner's private runtime, separate from general development and cloud-worker storage. No mailbox content, account credentials, or private assistant memory is published on this website. A host administrator can still access the underlying machine and its data.

The intended ongoing assistant route uses private inference. The owner may separately authorize cloud-assisted setup or share specific information for a particular task. That does not authorize blanket sharing of mailbox data or private memory with cloud workers. Relevant Google services necessarily process requests sent to their APIs.

Limits on use and sharing

Google user data is used for the owner's requested features, not for advertising, sale to third parties, or training general-purpose AI models. OpenClaw Private Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and owner controls

The owner controls locally retained information and can remove assistant data and credentials. Google authorization can be revoked in Google Account connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously retained local information. Deleting assistant data does not delete the original Gmail mailbox.

Backup and operational retention settings are still being configured. This notice will be updated if the installation's data practices change.

This informational website

These are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or assistant login. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information needed to serve the pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The owner maintains these pages in the site repository. Do not post private information or credentials in public issues.
