OpenClaw Private Assistant

A personal assistant being configured to help its owner manage email and everyday responsibilities.

What the Google connection is for

The assistant uses Google OAuth to verify the connected account and obtain the owner's permission for email features. These include reading mail, organizing the inbox, and preparing drafts as the corresponding capabilities are enabled.

Google may group multiple capabilities in one permission. A granted permission is not standing authorization to send messages or take every possible action. The assistant operates within the owner's instructions.

This is an information page, not the assistant

The application is a personal-use installation, not a service accepting public users. This website provides no sign-in, mailbox access, assistant interface, private memory, or account credentials.

The assistant and its stored personal information remain in a separate private runtime controlled by its owner.
